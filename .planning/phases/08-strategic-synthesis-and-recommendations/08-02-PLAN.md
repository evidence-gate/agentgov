6. **Risk Assessment Framework (## 8.11)**:
   - 4 risk categories: Timing, Competitive, Platform, Regulatory
   - Scoring: Impact (1-5) x Likelihood (1-5) = Risk Score (1-25)
   - Risk tolerance definition for solo/small team

7. **Timing Risks (## 8.12)**:
   - Risk T1: Market moves faster than solo developer can build (Impact 4, Likelihood 3)
     - Evidence: Phase 7 case studies show 3-year exit timelines, but competition window may be shorter
     - Mitigation: SDK-first launch (Phase 7 Pattern 1) gets to market in 4-8 weeks, not 6 months
   - Risk T2: EU AI Act enforcement delayed or weakened (Impact 3, Likelihood 2)
     - Evidence: Phase 6 notes EC already missed guidance deadline
     - Mitigation: HITL has non-regulatory demand too (approval fatigue pain score 100/125)
   - Risk T3: AAIF joint interoperability spec changes protocol landscape (Impact 3, Likelihood 3)
     - Evidence: Phase 1 AAIF spec expected Q3 2026
     - Mitigation: Build protocol-agnostic with abstraction layer

8. **Competitive Risks (## 8.13)**:
   - Risk C1: Funded startup enters cost control with $10M+ (Impact 5, Likelihood 3)
     - Evidence: Phase 3 shows category is vacant, attracting entrants
     - Mitigation: OSS community moat (Phase 6 section 6.4 data network effects), first-mover in community
   - Risk C2: Existing observability player (Datadog, Langfuse) adds cost enforcement feature (Impact 4, Likelihood 4)
     - Evidence: Phase 4 shows observability players track costs but don't enforce
     - Mitigation: Deep specialization > feature addition. Cost enforcement requires fundamentally different architecture
   - Risk C3: LangGraph or framework adds built-in HITL that's "good enough" (Impact 3, Likelihood 4)
     - Evidence: LangGraph interrupt() already exists (Phase 5)
     - Mitigation: Framework-agnostic positioning. interrupt() creates lock-in that enterprises resist

9. **Platform Risks (## 8.14)**:
   - Risk P1: Big Tech (AWS/Google/Azure) ships agent cost control as platform feature (Impact 5, Likelihood 2)
     - Evidence: Phase 4 Big Tech incentive misalignment analysis
     - Mitigation: Multi-cloud neutrality is inherent advantage (Phase 4 section 4.8)
   - Risk P2: Cloudflare Workers pricing changes or capability limitations (Impact 3, Likelihood 2)
     - Mitigation: Architecture should allow migration to alternative edge compute
   - Risk P3: MCP protocol breaking changes in SDK v2 (Impact 2, Likelihood 3)
     - Evidence: Phase 1 MCP roadmap
     - Mitigation: Abstraction layer over MCP SDK, track AAIF spec discussions

10. **Regulatory Risks (## 8.15)**:
    - Risk R1: EU AI Act high-risk scope interpreted more narrowly than expected (Impact 3, Likelihood 3)
      - Evidence: Phase 6 notes 18-40% of AI systems classified as high-risk (appliedAI survey)
      - Mitigation: Cost Control is the primary product; HITL is additive, not dependent
    - Risk R2: Japan regulatory environment becomes restrictive (Impact 2, Likelihood 1)
      - Evidence: Phase 6 Japan analysis -- AI Promotion Act is facilitative
      - Mitigation: Extremely unlikely. Japan's approach is promotion-first

11. **Risk Mitigation Summary Matrix (## 8.16)**:
    - All risks in a single matrix: Risk | Impact | Likelihood | Score | Mitigation | Owner
    - Top 5 risks highlighted

12. **Retreat Signals and Pivot Options (## 8.17)**:

    - When to shut down: if all 3 retreat signals trigger AND no pivot shows traction
