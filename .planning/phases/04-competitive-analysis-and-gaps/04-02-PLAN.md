Add a per-category OSS vs SaaS analysis section. For each of the 14 infrastructure categories, assess:

**Analysis Framework:**
1. **Current market split**: What % of existing players are OSS vs SaaS? Who is winning and why?
2. **Data sensitivity**: Does the category handle sensitive data that favors self-hosting (OSS) or managed services (SaaS)?
3. **Network effects**: Does the product improve with more users? (favors SaaS platform)
4. **Operational complexity**: How hard is self-hosting? (complex = SaaS advantage)
5. **Switching costs**: How sticky is the product once adopted? (high switching costs = SaaS defensible)
6. **Community value**: Does an OSS community create direct product value (plugins, integrations, bug reports)?
7. **Small team fit**: Can a 1-3 person team realistically maintain an OSS project in this category?

**Per-category output:**
- Current OSS/SaaS split among existing players
- Recommended approach for new entrant: OSS-first / SaaS-first / Hybrid
- Rationale (2-3 sentences)
- Defensibility factors: what creates moat in this category?

Create a summary table: Category x Recommended Approach x Key Defensibility Factor x Small Team Feasibility.

Write in Japanese, consistent with prior report sections.
