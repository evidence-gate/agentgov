Continue the report with detailed moat analysis for the top 3 categories:

**Category 5: Cost Control -- Moat Analysis**
1. **Data network effects**: Cost pattern data improves anomaly detection → more users → better alerts → stronger lock-in. Quantify: how much data is needed for meaningful anomaly detection?
2. **Switching costs**: Budget policies, cost history, alert configurations are hard to migrate. Compare to: Datadog's retention moat (customers don't switch because of historical data)
3. **Community moat**: Budget templates, framework integration plugins, cost optimization patterns contributed by community. Reference PostHog/Supabase community contribution rates
4. **OSS moat**: MIT license → maximum adoption → "buy-out currency" (Langfuse model). Self-host option as enterprise differentiator
5. **Time-to-build estimate**: What can 1 person build in 3/6/12 months? Milestones: MVP proxy → budget enforcement → multi-provider → dashboard → anomaly detection
6. **Japan-specific considerations**: Timezone advantage for async-first community, English-first product strategy, global-first market approach

**Category 11: HITL/Approval -- Moat Analysis**
1. **Regulatory expertise moat**: Deep understanding of EU AI Act Article 14 requirements → compliance templates that competitors must reverse-engineer
2. **Workflow complexity moat**: Approval workflows are organization-specific → deep customization → high switching costs
3. **Audit trail lock-in**: Historical approval records are compliance-critical → cannot easily migrate
4. **Integration depth**: Slack/Teams/Email/Discord integrations → the more channels connected, the harder to switch
5. **Time-to-build estimate**: MVP timeline for solo developer
6. **Japan-specific considerations**: EU regulatory expertise as differentiator, timezone coverage for EU/US markets

**Category 6: Error Handling (bundled) -- Moat Analysis**
1. **Pattern database moat**: Failure patterns accumulated from production deployments
2. **Why bundling with Cost Control is the moat strategy**: Standalone WTP is weak, but as part of "agent governance" the switching cost compounds
3. **Time-to-build estimate**: As a feature within cost control, not standalone

End with a comparative moat strength assessment and sources section.
